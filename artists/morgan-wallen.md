# Morgan Wallen

![Morgan Wallen Photo](/assets/images/morgan-wallen.jpg)

## About the artist
Morgan Cole Wallen (born May 13, 1993) is an American country music singer. He competed in the sixth season of The Voice, originally as a member of Usher's team, but later as a member of Adam Levine's team. After being eliminated in the playoffs of that season, he signed to Panacea Records, releasing his debut EP, Stand Alone, in 2015.
